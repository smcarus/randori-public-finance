# Plan, borrow and forecast for a Massachusetts town

Four linked tools built on Randori's planning models and the Division of Local Services templates. The school project set on the Tax bill tab carries into the capital plan, and the capital plan's debt service carries into the forecast.

**Illustrative only.** "Town of Example" is a hypothetical Massachusetts town of 30,000 with sample data. It is not affiliated with any real community or issuer, and nothing here is advice to any town. Randori Resources' municipal advisor registration is pending.

### Models and sources

- Engine: Randori Massachusetts Planning Models v1.0. School Building (Model Inventory M-06, v1.0) gives the MSBA grant, town share, note interest and bond payments. Debt Exclusion Impact (M-07, v1.0) turns them into the tax rate and average-home bill. The levy-limit comparison uses the Capital Plan levy projection (M-08, v1.0). The Capital plan tab mirrors the Capital Plan model (M-08, v1.0): existing debt, five proposed projects, and the four policy tests. M-09 to M-11 are not used here.
- The Five-year forecast follows the layout and sample growth assumptions of the DLS Financial Forecast template, with the levy limit computed as on the DLS Tax Levy worksheet. It is a new draft model with sample figures for the hypothetical town. It is not yet in the Model Inventory and has not been tested beyond its levy limit. The Budget calendar uses the DLS Budget to Tax Rate Recap Calendar template.
- Massachusetts Division of Local Services templates: the commercial shift uses the residential-factor formula and minimum factor from the DLS Tax Rate Options worksheet; the maximum allowable levy follows the DLS levy limit worksheet (levy limit plus debt exclusions); bond payments were cross-checked against the DLS Debt Service Calculator; the 30-year term cap follows the DLS Asset Useful Life Schedules and Maximum Borrowing Terms (August 11, 2017).
- The Model Inventory records M-06 to M-08 as tested on October 7, 2026 (Pass). All three still run on sample data for a hypothetical town. Checked on October 13, 2026 with no differences above one cent: tax bill against the workbook (six scenarios, 630 values); capital plan against M-08 (two scenarios, 330 values); bond payments against the DLS Debt Service Calculator (12 values); forecast levy limit against the DLS forecast template (5 years).
- Conventions from the workbook: fiscal years end June 30; full-year interest; note interest charged on the year-end balance (conservative); MSBA pays progress payments with a one-month lag; eligible costs are spent in step with the total budget.
- Ballot wording, borrowing authorizations and use of premium must be confirmed with bond counsel. Actual tax rates come from the community's DLS tax rate recapitulation.
